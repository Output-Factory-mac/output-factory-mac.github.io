Output Factory for Mac

Automate printing and exporting from Adobe InDesign. Save hours on repetitive tasks with batch processing, layer versioning, and automatic preflighting. Select your documents, set preferences, and let Output Factory handle the rest—in the background, overnight, or while you work elsewhere.

Batch Output at Scale

Process dozens of InDesign files with one click. Supports print, PDF, PostScript, EPS, JPEG, TIFF, IDML, EPUB, HTML, and more. Export as single pages, page groups, or multiple formats simultaneously.

Layer Versioning and Variables
